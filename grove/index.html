Neighborhood standards

Designed for The Grove from the first sketch.

The Grove’s covenants keep the neighborhood beautiful, and every Keystone design starts from them. The highlights:

  • Homes of at least 3,000 square feet of heated living space on lots 1 to 15, and 3,500 square feet on lots 16 to 40.
  • Exteriors at least 80% brick or masonry, with main roofs at a 9/12 pitch or steeper.
  • Generous setbacks: 75 feet in front, 30 feet at the rear and 20 feet at each side.
  • Plans, finishes and landscaping are approved by the neighborhood’s Architectural Review Committee. We prepare the submission for you.

This is a summary; the recorded declaration governs. Read the full covenants (PDF).

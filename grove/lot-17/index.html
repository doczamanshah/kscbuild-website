The residence

Classic in form. Filled with light.

Cream brick, black-framed windows and a steep charcoal roofline give this home the quiet confidence of a classic. Step through the glass double doors and it opens up: a two-story living room rises the full height of the house, washed in light from windows above and below.

Every day is planned for ease. The owner’s suite and a private guest suite are both on the main level, so the home lives beautifully on one floor, with two more bedrooms upstairs for family and friends.

The homesite

Room to breathe, and a street that stays beautiful.

Lot 17 sits within The Grove, a gated neighborhood of custom homes where deep front lawns and generous side yards are part of the design. A 75-foot front setback gives the home a proper approach: a long drive, a manicured lawn, and a facade you see whole from the street.

Designed for The Grove

The neighborhood standards, built in from the first sketch.

The recorded covenants keep The Grove beautiful. This home is designed to meet every one of them, and we handle the approvals for you.

Living space

At least 3,500 square feet heated, not counting basement, porches or garage.

Exterior

At least 80% brick or masonry, with foundations faced in the same masonry.

Roof

Architectural shingles, main roofs at a 9/12 pitch or steeper.

Setbacks

75 feet in front, 30 feet at the rear and 20 feet at each side.

Approvals

Plans, colors and landscaping approved by the Architectural Review Committee. We prepare the submission.

Finish

Driveway, sod and five new trees completed with the home.

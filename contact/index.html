Good to know

Questions people ask before they call.

Is the first conversation free?

Yes. Initial conversations are free, with no obligation. We will listen, learn what matters most to you, and suggest a sensible next step.

How quickly will I hear back?

We reply to every inquiry within one business day, by phone or email, whichever you prefer.

What kinds of projects does Keystone take on?

New custom homes, renovations and additions, residential developments, and commercial spaces, including medical office buildings such as Shoals Medical Park in Sheffield.

Where does Keystone build?

We are based in Florence, Alabama and work across the Shoals, including Muscle Shoals, Sheffield, and Tuscumbia, and in surrounding North Alabama communities.

What should I bring to a first meeting?

Whatever you have: a site or lot, a sketch, saved inspiration, existing plans, or simply a feeling about how you want the space to look and work.
